# Cinacoin Developer Ambassador — Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist to track your progress through the first 30 days as a Cinacoin Developer Ambassador. Your community manager will review this with you at the end of your onboarding period.

---

## Week 1: Welcome & Setup

### Account & Access

- [ ] Welcome email received and reviewed
- [ ] Ambassador Discord role assigned
- [ ] Added to **Private Ambassador Channel** in Discord
- [ ] Added to ambassador mailing list / newsletter
- [ ] GitHub org invitation accepted (if applicable)
- [ ] Access to shared drive / resource folder confirmed

### Orientation

- [ ] Read the [Ambassador Program Guide](./ambassador-program.md)
- [ ] Review [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- [ ] Review [Contributing Guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [ ] Complete onboarding session with community manager
- [ ] Introduce yourself in the ambassador Discord channel

### Brand & Resources

- [ ] Download brand assets (logos, templates, style guide)
- [ ] Confirm swag kit shipping address
- [ ] Bookmark key resources:
  - [ ] [API Documentation](../api/)
  - [ ] [Developer Guides](../guides/)
  - [ ] [Examples](../examples/)
  - [ ] [FAQ](../faq.md)

---

## Week 2: First Contribution

### Planning

- [ ] Meet with community manager to define 30-day goals
- [ ] Choose your first contribution type:
  - [ ] Tutorial / guide
  - [ ] Integration / tool
  - [ ] Talk / presentation
  - [ ] Documentation contribution
  - [ ] Community help (answering questions, reviewing PRs)

### Preparation

- [ ] Review [Tutorial Writing Template](./ambassador-program.md#tutorial-writing-template) (if applicable)
- [ ] Review [Demo App Showcase Guidelines](./ambassador-program.md#demo-app-showcase-guidelines) (if applicable)
- [ ] Review [Integration Building Best Practices](./ambassador-program.md#integration-building-best-practices) (if applicable)
- [ ] Set up local development environment for Cinacoin
- [ ] Identify relevant core team contacts for questions

### Execution

- [ ] Draft / build first contribution
- [ ] Share draft in ambassador channel for feedback
- [ ] Incorporate feedback from core team or peers
- [ ] Publish contribution (PR, blog post, video, talk, etc.)

---

## Week 3: Community Engagement

### Participation

- [ ] Answer at least 3 questions in public Discord channels or forums
- [ ] Review at least 1 community PR or issue
- [ ] Attend a community call or event (if available)
- [ ] Share your first contribution on social media (tag Cinacoin)

### Feedback

- [ ] Request feedback on your first contribution from community manager
- [ ] Document what went well and what could be improved
- [ ] Adjust your approach for the next contribution

### Networking

- [ ] Connect with at least 2 other ambassadors
- [ ] Identify a potential collaboration (co-authored tutorial, joint talk, etc.)
- [ ] Join any relevant working groups or special interest channels

---

## Week 4: Review & Planning

### Reflection

- [ ] Complete self-assessment:
  - What did I accomplish this month?
  - What challenges did I face?
  - What support do I need from the core team?
- [ ] Update your contribution tracker

### 30-Day Review

- [ ] Schedule 30-day check-in with community manager
- [ ] Review checklist completion with community manager
- [ ] Discuss tier placement and trajectory (see [Incentives Structure](./ambassador-program.md#incentives-structure))
- [ ] Set goals for the next 60 days

### Looking Ahead

- [ ] Plan next 2–3 contributions
- [ ] Identify skill areas to develop (technical, communication, etc.)
- [ ] Explore speaking or event opportunities (if interested)
- [ ] Consider mentoring a new ambassador once established

---

## Ongoing Expectations (Trial Period: Days 30–90)

- [ ] Minimum 1 contribution per month
- [ ] Active participation in ambassador and public community channels
- [ ] Monthly check-in with community manager (brief async update)
- [ ] Attend quarterly ambassador sync call
- [ ] Maintain professionalism and accuracy in all public representations of Cinacoin
