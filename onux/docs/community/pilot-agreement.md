# Cinacoin Pilot Agreement

**Between:** CinaGroup ("Cinacoin")  
**And:** {{Organization Name}} ("Pilot Team")  
**Effective Date:** {{Date}}  
**Pilot Duration:** 4 weeks from Effective Date

## 1. Purpose

This agreement outlines the terms of a technical pilot program in which the Pilot Team integrates the Cinacoin SDK into their application, with dedicated support from the Cinacoin team.

## 2. Scope

The Pilot Team agrees to:
- Integrate Cinacoin SDK into their {{application name/description}}
- Use Cinacoin for {{specific features — e.g., wallet connection, multi-chain auth, cross-chain payments}}
- Provide feedback on SDK usability, documentation, and developer experience
- Participate in a post-pilot review session

Cinacoin agrees to:
- Provide a named technical contact for the duration of the pilot
- Respond to technical questions within 4 business hours
- Resolve pilot-related bugs with P0/P1 priority
- Evaluate pilot-identified feature requests for upcoming releases
- Provide early access to relevant unreleased features

## 3. Support Package

- **Communication:** Dedicated Slack/Discord channel or equivalent
- **Response Time:** < 4 hours for technical questions, < 24 hours for bug reports
- **Availability:** Business hours UTC+8 to UTC+0
- **Bug Priority:** Pilot-related bugs escalated to P0/P1
- **Feature Requests:** Evaluated for next release cycle

## 4. Terms

- **No Cost:** The pilot program is provided at no charge
- **No Exclusivity:** Either party may use other solutions concurrently
- **No Lock-in:** The Pilot Team may discontinue Cinacoin usage at any time
- **Open Source:** Cinacoin is provided under its existing open-source license (see repository)
- **Data:** The Pilot Team retains full ownership of their user data

## 5. Intellectual Property

- Cinacoin SDK remains the property of CinaGroup under its open-source license
- The Pilot Team's application and code remain their sole property
- Any feedback provided by the Pilot Team may be incorporated into Cinacoin without additional compensation or obligation

## 6. Co-Marketing (Optional)

Both parties agree to explore the following upon successful pilot completion:
- Joint announcement (social media, blog post)
- Technical case study (both parties review and approve before publication)
- Speaking opportunity at relevant conferences or community events
- Inclusion in Cinacoin's official documentation/examples (with attribution)

Co-marketing is optional and requires separate written agreement on specific activities.

## 7. Confidentiality

- Neither party will disclose the other's confidential information without permission
- Technical details shared during the pilot are confidential unless explicitly agreed otherwise
- Public announcements require mutual consent

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 7 days' written notice. Upon termination:
- Cinacoin support obligations end
- The Pilot Team may continue using the open-source SDK under its license
- Confidentiality obligations survive termination

## 9. Success Metrics

The pilot is considered successful when:
- Cinacoin is integrated into the Pilot Team's application within 4 weeks
- The integration is deployed to a production or staging environment
- No critical (P0) bugs remain unresolved at pilot conclusion
- The Pilot Team provides positive developer feedback (target: ≥ 4/5 rating)
