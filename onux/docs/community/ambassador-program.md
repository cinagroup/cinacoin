# Cinacoin Developer Ambassador Program

## Program Overview

The Cinacoin Developer Ambassador Program is designed to grow Cinacoin adoption through passionate community advocates who share their knowledge, build integrations, and help onboard new developers into the ecosystem. Our mission is to create a decentralized network of technical leaders who can amplify Cinacoin's reach, improve developer experience, and drive real-world usage.

**What Ambassadors Do:**

- **Write Tutorials** — Create clear, practical guides for building with Cinacoin, from beginner walkthroughs to advanced integration patterns
- **Give Talks** — Present at meetups, conferences, hackathons, and livestreams about Cinacoin's technology and use cases
- **Help Newcomers** — Answer questions in community forums, Discord channels, and GitHub discussions. Be the friendly face that makes onboarding smoother
- **Build Integrations** — Develop SDKs, plugins, tools, and starter projects that extend Cinacoin's ecosystem and make development easier for everyone

**Benefits of Being an Ambassador:**

- Early access to new features, APIs, and unreleased documentation
- Exclusive Cinacoin swag (hoodies, stickers, hardware wallets, and more)
- Public recognition on the Cinacoin website, social media, and documentation
- Potential compensation through our tiered incentive program (see below)
- Direct line to the core team for feedback, feature requests, and collaboration

## Eligibility Requirements

To become a Cinacoin Developer Ambassador, candidates should meet the following criteria:

**Technical Proficiency:**

- Demonstrated experience with web3/blockchain development (Solidity, smart contracts, dApps, DeFi protocols, or related)
- Comfortable reading and contributing to open-source projects
- Understanding of blockchain fundamentals: consensus, wallets, transactions, gas, tokenomics

**Communication Skills:**

- Strong technical writing ability — capable of producing clear tutorials and documentation
- Public speaking or video creation experience (or strong willingness to develop these skills)
- Able to explain complex concepts in accessible language for developers at all levels

**Community Engagement:**

- Active participation in developer communities (Discord, forums, GitHub, Stack Overflow, Twitter/X, etc.)
- A track record of helping others learn and solve problems
- Respectful, constructive communication aligned with our Code of Conduct

**Passion for Cinacoin's Mission:**

- Genuine enthusiasm for Cinacoin's vision and technology
- Alignment with our values of decentralization, openness, and developer empowerment
- Willingness to represent Cinacoin professionally in public settings

## Application Process

Becoming a Cinacoin Developer Ambassador is a structured process designed to ensure mutual fit and set both you and the core team up for success.

### Step 1: Submit Your Application

Fill out our [Developer Ambassador Application Form](https://forms.google.com/cinacoin-ambassador) (placeholder link — update when form is live). The form collects:

- Your name, contact info, and social/GitHub profiles
- A brief description of your background and experience with web3/blockchain
- Links to writing samples, tutorials, talks, or open-source contributions
- What excites you about Cinacoin and what you'd like to contribute
- Your availability and preferred activities (writing, speaking, building, mentoring)

### Step 2: Interview with Core Team

If your application passes initial review, a member of the core team will schedule a 30-minute video interview. This is a two-way conversation — we want to learn about your goals, and you should feel free to ask about the program, expectations, and support we provide.

### Step 3: Onboarding Session

Accepted candidates attend a guided onboarding session covering:

- Program structure, expectations, and communication channels
- Access to private resources (Discord channels, documentation repos, etc.)
- Introduction to your ambassador cohort and community manager
- Goal-setting for your first 90 days

### Step 4: 3-Month Trial Period

All ambassadors begin with a 3-month trial period. During this time:

- You'll work on agreed-upon contributions (tutorials, talks, integrations)
- The core team provides support, feedback, and resources
- At the end of 90 days, both sides evaluate the fit and decide on continued participation

## Onboarding Guide

Congratulations on joining the Cinacoin Developer Ambassador Program! This guide will help you get started quickly and effectively.

### Welcome Packet

Upon acceptance, you'll receive a welcome packet containing:

- **Program Handbook** — Detailed overview of expectations, guidelines, and resources
- **Brand Assets** — Cinacoin logos, color palettes, and presentation templates for your use in tutorials, talks, and content
- **Swag Kit** — We'll ship your ambassador welcome kit (hoodie, stickers, pins) to your address
- **Contact Directory** — Key contacts on the core team, your cohort members, and community managers

### Private Discord Access

You'll be added to the **Private Ambassador Channel** in the Cinacoin Discord server. This is your space for:

- Direct communication with the core team and fellow ambassadors
- Early announcements about feature launches and program updates
- Collaborative brainstorming for content and integration ideas
- Feedback and support requests

Please review the Discord community guidelines and ambassador-specific code of conduct upon joining.

### Documentation Contribution Guidelines

Ambassadors are encouraged to contribute to Cinacoin's documentation:

1. **Fork the docs repo** and create a feature branch for your changes
2. Follow the [documentation style guide](../guides/docs-style-guide.md) for consistent formatting and tone
3. Submit a PR with a clear description of your changes
4. Tag `@community` for review — ambassador PRs receive priority review

All contributions must be technically accurate, include code examples where appropriate, and link to relevant API references.

### Tutorial Writing Template

When creating tutorials, follow this structure:

1. **Title** — Clear, descriptive, and searchable
2. **Prerequisites** — What the reader needs to know or have installed
3. **Learning Objectives** — 2–4 bullet points of what they'll accomplish
4. **Step-by-Step Instructions** — Numbered steps with code blocks and expected outputs
5. **Complete Code Example** — Link to a working repo or gist
6. **Troubleshooting** — Common pitfalls and solutions
7. **Next Steps** — Links to related tutorials, API docs, or community resources

Submit tutorials to the community docs repo or publish on your own blog and share the link with the core team.

### Demo App Showcase Guidelines

When building demo applications to showcase Cinacoin:

- Keep the scope focused — demonstrate one or two key features clearly rather than many features superficially
- Include a README with setup instructions, architecture overview, and Cinacoin-specific implementation notes
- Deploy to a public URL when possible (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, etc.)
- Tag your repo with `cinacoin`, `demo`, and relevant technology tags
- Submit to the [Cinacoin Ecosystem Directory](./ecosystem-directory.md) for inclusion

### Integration Building Best Practices

When building integrations (SDKs, plugins, tools):

- Start by reviewing existing integrations to avoid duplication
- Follow Cinacoin's API versioning and deprecation policies
- Write comprehensive tests and include CI/CD configuration
- Publish to appropriate package registries (npm, PyPI, crates.io, etc.)
- Maintain your integration or coordinate handoff if you can no longer support it

## Incentives Structure

The Cinacoin Ambassador Program uses a tiered incentive system that rewards impact, consistency, and community contribution. Progression between tiers is based on measurable performance metrics reviewed quarterly.

### Tier 1 — Community Contributor

**Value:** ~$50 (recognition + swag)

**Requirements:**

- Complete onboarding and publish first contribution within 30 days
- Minimum 1 tutorial, talk, or integration per quarter
- Active participation in community channels

**Perks:**

- Public recognition on the Cinacoin website and social media
- Ambassador badge on GitHub/Discord
- Cinacoin swag pack (hoodie, stickers, pins)
- Access to private ambassador Discord channel

### Tier 2 — Active Ambassador

**Value:** $200/month stipend + early access

**Requirements:**

- Sustained Tier 1 performance for at least 1 quarter
- Minimum 2 contributions per quarter (tutorials, talks, integrations, or significant community help)
- Positive feedback from community members and core team

**Perks:**

- Everything in Tier 1, plus:
- $200/month stipend (paid in stablecoin or fiat)
- Early access to new features, APIs, and beta programs
- Direct Slack/Discord line to core team engineers
- Invitation to quarterly ambassador sync calls

### Tier 3 — Senior Ambassador

**Value:** $1,000/quarter bonus + speaking opportunities

**Requirements:**

- Sustained Tier 2 performance for at least 2 consecutive quarters
- Minimum 4 high-impact contributions per quarter
- Demonstrated leadership (mentoring new ambassadors, organizing events, building major integrations)
- Strong positive impact on community growth metrics

**Perks:**

- Everything in Tier 2, plus:
- $1,000/quarter performance bonus
- Sponsored speaking opportunities at major conferences
- Co-authorship opportunities on technical blog posts and whitepapers
- Advisory input on product roadmap and developer experience

### Performance Metrics

Ambassador progression and tier placement are evaluated based on:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Tutorial Views | Page views, engagement time, and completion rates for tutorials |
| Integration Usage | Downloads, active users, and GitHub stars for integrations you build |
| Community Help | Helpful answers in Discord/forums, issues resolved, PRs reviewed |
| Event Impact | Attendees at talks, feedback scores, and post-event engagement |
| Content Quality | Core team review of technical accuracy, clarity, and completeness |
